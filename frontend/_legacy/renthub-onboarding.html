Verify your Identity

A valid government ID and a selfie are both required. Your selfie must match the photo on your ID.

  • Photograph the whole ID in good light, with no glare, flat on a table.
  • The picture, the ID name and the expiry date (passport and driver's licence) must be easy to read.
  • Student cards, work IDs, bank cards and ordinary pictures are not accepted.
  • Use your own ID. The selfie is compared with the photo on it.
